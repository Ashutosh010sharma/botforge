Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

Welcome to BotForge. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect your information when you use our platform, website, and AI chatbot services.

By creating an account or using BotForge, you agree to the collection and use of information as described in this policy. If you do not agree with any part of this policy, please do not use our platform.

1. Information We Collect

We collect the following types of information when you use BotForge:

Account Information

  • Full name and email address provided during registration.
  • Password (stored in encrypted form — we never store plain-text passwords).

Company Information

  • Company name, website URL, industry, and business description provided during workspace setup.

Chatbot Data

  • Bot names, descriptions, welcome messages, and configuration settings.
  • Website content collected during AI training.
  • Visitor conversations stored in Chat Logs.

Usage Data

  • Subscription usage metrics including bots created, pages crawled, and AI messages processed.

2. How We Use Your Information

BotForge uses the information we collect to:

  • Create and manage your account and company workspace.
  • Train and operate your AI chatbots using your website content.
  • Process AI responses for visitor conversations.
  • Monitor subscription usage and enforce plan limits.
  • Provide customer support and respond to inquiries.
  • Improve the platform based on usage patterns and feedback.
  • Send important account and service notifications.
We do not sell your personal information to third parties. Your data is used solely to operate and improve the BotForge platform.

3. Website Crawling & Training Data

When you train a chatbot, BotForge automatically crawls publicly accessible pages of the website URL you provide. The content collected during crawling is used exclusively to build your chatbot's knowledge base.

  • Only publicly accessible pages are processed — password-protected or private pages are not collected.
  • Crawled content is stored in an isolated knowledge base specific to your chatbot.
  • Your training data is never shared with or used by other users' chatbots.
  • You may remove crawled content at any time by deleting your chatbot's knowledge base.

4. Data Storage & Security

BotForge takes reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your data from unauthorized access, loss, or misuse.

  • All communication between your browser and BotForge uses HTTPS encryption.
  • Passwords are stored using strong cryptographic hashing — plain-text passwords are never stored.
  • Each company workspace is isolated — data from one account cannot be accessed by another.
  • Chatbot knowledge bases are stored securely and accessed only during live conversations.
While we implement industry-standard security measures, no system is completely immune to risk. We encourage you to use strong passwords and keep your account credentials secure.

5. Cookies & Local Storage

BotForge and the chat widget use browser-based storage to improve the user experience.

  • Session Cookies: Used to maintain your login session on the BotForge dashboard.
  • Widget Local Storage: The chat widget stores a unique session identifier in the visitor's browser to maintain conversation continuity during a single visit.

These identifiers do not contain personally identifiable information and are used only to associate chat messages within the same session.

6. Third-Party Services

BotForge uses third-party services to operate the platform. These services may process data as part of delivering AI capabilities, hosting, and infrastructure.

  • AI Providers: Visitor questions and relevant context are processed by AI language model providers to generate responses.
  • Cloud Hosting: BotForge is hosted on cloud infrastructure providers that adhere to industry-standard security practices.

We work only with reputable third-party providers and take reasonable steps to ensure your data is handled responsibly.

7. Data Retention

We retain your data for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide services.

  • Account Data: Retained while your account remains active.
  • Chatbot Knowledge: Retained until you delete the chatbot or remove the knowledge base.
  • Chat Logs: Retained until you clear them from the Danger Zone settings.

You may request deletion of your account and associated data at any time by contacting support.

8. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Access: Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Correction: Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete information.
  • Deletion: Request deletion of your account and associated data.
  • Portability: Request your data in a structured, commonly used format.
  • Objection: Object to specific uses of your data where applicable.

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us using the information provided below.

9. Children's Privacy

BotForge is intended for use by businesses and is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or for legal, operational, or regulatory reasons. When we update this policy, we will revise the "Last updated" date at the top of this page.

We encourage you to review this policy periodically. Your continued use of BotForge after any changes indicates your acceptance of the updated policy.

11. Contact Us

If you have any questions, concerns, or requests regarding this Privacy Policy or how we handle your data, please contact us.
