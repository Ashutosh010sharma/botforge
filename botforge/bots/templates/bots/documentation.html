Chapter 01

Introduction

Overview of BotForge

Welcome to BotForge — a platform designed to help businesses create intelligent AI-powered chatbots trained using their own website content. With BotForge, you can deploy a customized chatbot on your website without writing complex AI code or managing machine learning models.

Whether you are a startup, a growing business, or an enterprise organization, BotForge provides a simple workflow that allows you to create, train, deploy, and manage AI assistants from a single dashboard.

What is BotForge?

BotForge is a Software as a Service (SaaS) platform that enables organizations to build conversational AI assistants using the information already available on their websites.

Instead of manually creating hundreds of questions and answers, BotForge automatically crawls your website, extracts meaningful content, processes the information into an AI knowledge base, and allows your chatbot to answer visitor questions using that knowledge.

The platform focuses on making AI deployment simple while providing complete control over your chatbot, its training data, installation, and usage.

Why Choose BotForge?

Modern businesses receive the same customer questions repeatedly:

  • What services do you provide?
  • How can customers contact you?
  • What are your business hours?
  • What products are available?
  • Where are you located?
  • What pricing plans do you offer?

Instead of requiring a human to answer these questions repeatedly, BotForge allows your chatbot to answer them instantly using information collected from your own website. The result is a faster customer experience while reducing support workload.

Core Features

BotForge includes everything required to create and manage business AI assistants.

AI Chatbot Creation

Create multiple AI chatbots for different projects or websites directly from your dashboard.

Website Knowledge Training

Train your chatbot by crawling your website automatically. BotForge discovers pages and converts them into searchable AI knowledge.

Smart Website Crawling

The crawler follows internal links, discovers additional pages, reads content, and builds a structured knowledge base.

Knowledge Management

Manage the knowledge used by your chatbot from a dedicated Knowledge Base section inside each bot.

Easy Installation

Deploy your chatbot by copying a single JavaScript snippet into your website. No server configuration required.

Chat Logs

Monitor conversations between visitors and your chatbot to understand questions and improve your content.

Billing & Usage

Monitor your subscription plan, bot limits, page limits, and usage statistics through the Billing Dashboard.

How BotForge Works

The BotForge workflow follows a simple process.

1 Create Company
2 Choose Subscription Plan
3 Create Chatbot
4 Train Using Website
5 Generate Knowledge Base
6 Install Widget
7 Visitors Start Chatting

Once installed, your chatbot answers visitor questions using the knowledge collected from your website.

Platform Workflow

Every chatbot created in BotForge follows the same lifecycle.

1

Create Your Workspace

Begin by creating your company workspace. Your workspace stores your bots, subscription, website information, and AI knowledge.

2

Select a Subscription

Choose a subscription plan based on the number of bots and website pages you wish to manage.

3

Create a Bot

Create a chatbot by providing basic information such as the bot name, website URL, and welcome message.

4

Train Your Bot

BotForge crawls your website and builds an AI knowledge base from the discovered pages.

5

Install the Widget

Copy the installation script and paste it into your website. Your chatbot becomes available immediately.

Typical Use Cases

BotForge can be used across multiple industries. Examples include:

Business websites IT companies Digital agencies Schools Universities Healthcare providers Law firms Marketing agencies Product companies SaaS platforms Real estate Financial services Consulting firms

Any website containing useful information can become a knowledge source for your chatbot.

Supported Website Types

BotForge works with most publicly accessible websites. Examples include:

HTML websites PHP websites WordPress Laravel Django React applications Next.js websites Shopify stores Bootstrap websites

As long as the content is publicly available, BotForge can use it during website training.

Benefits of Using BotForge

Using BotForge provides several advantages.

  • Reduce repetitive customer support questions.
  • Provide instant responses to website visitors.
  • Make business information available 24/7.
  • Improve visitor engagement.
  • Increase lead generation opportunities.
  • Deploy AI without machine learning expertise.
  • Manage multiple chatbots from one dashboard.
  • Monitor usage and conversations easily.

Before You Begin

Before creating your first chatbot, ensure you have:

Checklist: a registered BotForge account, your company workspace configured, an active subscription plan, and a publicly accessible website. These requirements allow BotForge to successfully train and deploy your AI chatbot.
