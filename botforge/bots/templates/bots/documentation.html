Chapter 01

Introduction

Overview of BotForge

Welcome to BotForge — a platform designed to help businesses create intelligent AI-powered chatbots trained using their own website content. With BotForge, you can deploy a customized chatbot on your website without writing complex AI code or managing machine learning models.

Whether you are a startup, a growing business, or an enterprise organization, BotForge provides a simple workflow that allows you to create, train, deploy, and manage AI assistants from a single dashboard.

What is BotForge?

BotForge is a Software as a Service (SaaS) platform that enables organizations to build conversational AI assistants using the information already available on their websites.

Instead of manually creating hundreds of questions and answers, BotForge automatically crawls your website, extracts meaningful content, processes the information into an AI knowledge base, and allows your chatbot to answer visitor questions using that knowledge.

The platform focuses on making AI deployment simple while providing complete control over your chatbot, its training data, installation, and usage.

Why Choose BotForge?

Modern businesses receive the same customer questions repeatedly:

  • What services do you provide?
  • How can customers contact you?
  • What are your business hours?
  • What products are available?
  • Where are you located?
  • What pricing plans do you offer?

Instead of requiring a human to answer these questions repeatedly, BotForge allows your chatbot to answer them instantly using information collected from your own website. The result is a faster customer experience while reducing support workload.

Core Features

BotForge includes everything required to create and manage business AI assistants.

AI Chatbot Creation

Create multiple AI chatbots for different projects or websites directly from your dashboard.

Website Knowledge Training

Train your chatbot by crawling your website automatically. BotForge discovers pages and converts them into searchable AI knowledge.

Smart Website Crawling

The crawler follows internal links, discovers additional pages, reads content, and builds a structured knowledge base.

Knowledge Management

Manage the knowledge used by your chatbot from a dedicated Knowledge Base section inside each bot.

Easy Installation

Deploy your chatbot by copying a single JavaScript snippet into your website. No server configuration required.

Chat Logs

Monitor conversations between visitors and your chatbot to understand questions and improve your content.

Billing & Usage

Monitor your subscription plan, bot limits, page limits, and usage statistics through the Billing Dashboard.

Why Company Setup is Required

The company workspace is the foundation of your BotForge account. Instead of creating chatbots directly under a user account, BotForge organizes everything inside a company workspace — ensuring bots, subscription plans, knowledge, and analytics remain connected to your business.

  • Organize all chatbots in one place.
  • Connect your official business website.
  • Manage subscription plans.
  • Track chatbot usage.
  • Prepare your account for future business features.
Important: Without a company workspace, chatbot creation is disabled. You must complete this step before doing anything else.

Chapter 4 — Subscription Plans

Chapter 04

Subscription Plans

Choose the right plan for your business

BotForge offers flexible subscription plans designed to meet the needs of businesses of all sizes. Whether you are creating a single chatbot or managing multiple AI assistants, selecting the right subscription ensures your workspace has the resources it needs.

Why a Subscription is Required

Every BotForge workspace requires an active subscription before chatbots can be created. AI chatbots require computing resources to operate. Your subscription allocates those resources based on your expected usage.

Crawl Websites

Resources are needed to discover and process your website pages.

Process Knowledge

Content is cleaned, chunked, and converted into AI embeddings.

Generate Responses

Each visitor conversation consumes AI processing resources.

Store Embeddings

Vector knowledge bases are stored and queried for every response.

No active subscription = no chatbot creation. BotForge prevents chatbot creation until a subscription is activated to ensure every workspace operates within its assigned resource limits.

What Each Plan Includes

Every subscription displays its available resources before selection. The three key limits every plan defines are:

Maximum AI Chatbots

The maximum number of chatbots your company can create under the selected plan. If you attempt to create more bots than your plan allows, BotForge will notify you and recommend upgrading.

Starter Plan  →  Maximum Bots: 2

Website Training Pages

Defines how many website pages BotForge can crawl and process during AI training. Larger websites generally require higher plans to allow more pages to be processed.

Maximum Website Pages  →  250 Pages

Monthly AI Messages

Every visitor conversation consumes AI messages. Your subscription includes a monthly allowance. Monitoring message usage helps ensure uninterrupted chatbot availability.

Monthly Messages  →  25,000 Messages

Choosing Your First Plan

After completing your company setup, BotForge redirects you to the Pricing page. Follow these steps to activate your workspace:

1

Review the Available Plans

Each plan card shows the included resource limits clearly.

2

Compare the Resources

Compare max bots, training pages, and monthly messages across plans.

3

Click Get Started

Click Get Started on your preferred plan and confirm your selection.

4

Workspace Activated

Once activated, the subscription becomes the active plan for your workspace immediately.

Selecting the Right Plan

When selecting a plan, consider the following factors:

  • Number of websites you need to train.
  • Size of your website (number of pages).
  • Expected monthly visitor traffic.
  • Estimated number of monthly conversations.
  • Future business growth and expansion.
A small business may only require one chatbot and limited pages. Growing businesses often benefit from higher limits — multiple chatbots, larger knowledge bases, and more monthly conversations.

Current Active Plan

Only one subscription can be active for a company at a time. The active plan is displayed throughout your dashboard. Your Billing Dashboard shows:

Plan Name

Your currently active subscription tier.

Monthly Price

The cost of your current plan per billing cycle.

Active Status

Confirms whether your subscription is currently active.

Current Usage

Real-time progress against your plan's resource limits.

Upgrading Your Subscription

As your business grows, your AI requirements may increase. BotForge allows you to upgrade at any time — upgrading immediately increases your available resource limits without affecting existing chatbots.

Create additional chatbots Train larger websites More monthly conversations Expand business operations

Downgrading Your Subscription

If your business requirements decrease, you may choose a lower plan. Before downgrading, ensure your current usage does not exceed the new plan's limits.

Check before downgrading: Your chatbot count, website pages used, and monthly message requirements must all fit within the new plan's limits. Planning ahead ensures a smooth transition.

Plan Limits

BotForge automatically monitors your subscription limits and notifies you as you approach them.

Bot Limit Reached

BotForge prevents additional chatbot creation and prompts you to upgrade your subscription.

Page Limit Reached

The website crawler stops after reaching your page limit. Upgrading your subscription increases this cap.

Message Limit Reached

Once the monthly message allowance is consumed, BotForge notifies you through the Billing Dashboard.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I use BotForge without a subscription?
No. A company must have an active subscription before creating chatbots.
Can I upgrade my plan later?
Yes. You can upgrade your subscription whenever your business requires additional resources.
What happens if I reach my chatbot limit?
BotForge prevents additional chatbot creation and displays an upgrade option.
Does upgrading affect my existing bots?
No. Existing chatbots continue working normally. Only your available resource limits increase.

Chapter 8 — Knowledge Base

Chapter 08

Knowledge Base

Train your bot using your website

The Knowledge Base is the intelligence behind your chatbot. When a visitor asks a question, BotForge searches the knowledge base to find the most relevant information collected from your website before generating a response. A well-trained knowledge base leads to more accurate, reliable, and context-aware conversations.

What is the Knowledge Base?

The Knowledge Base is a structured collection of information extracted from your website. During website training, BotForge processes your content and converts it into AI-searchable knowledge — allowing your chatbot to answer questions using your own business information instead of generic responses.

Crawls Website

Automatically discovers and visits all publicly accessible internal pages.

Extracts Content

Pulls meaningful text — headings, paragraphs, lists, product info, FAQs.

Cleans & Organizes

Removes navigation, scripts, CSS, and decorative elements — keeping only useful content.

Builds AI Index

Converts cleaned content into a searchable knowledge index your chatbot queries in real time.

Why is the Knowledge Base Important?

Without knowledge, your chatbot cannot answer business-specific questions. Visitors regularly ask things like:

  • What services do you provide?
  • What are your pricing plans?
  • Where is your office located?
  • How do I contact support?
  • What are your business hours?
These answers come directly from your website content. The more complete and accurate your website, the better your chatbot performs.

Opening the Knowledge Base

1

Open Bot Workspace

Navigate to Bots and click Workspace on the chatbot you want to train.

2

Click Knowledge Base Tab

Select the Knowledge Base tab from the workspace navigation.

3

Knowledge Base Dashboard Opens

From here you can start training, view crawling status, and manage your chatbot's knowledge.

Website Training Pipeline

Website training is the process of teaching your chatbot using your website content. The entire pipeline runs automatically — you simply provide the website URL.

1 Website URL
2 Website Crawling
3 Content Extraction
4 Content Cleaning
5 Knowledge Processing
6 AI Search Index Built
7 Chatbot Ready

What Content is Collected?

BotForge focuses on meaningful content that helps answer visitor questions.

Collected

Page titles · Headings · Paragraphs · Lists · Tables · Product & service descriptions · FAQs · Contact information

Ignored

Navigation menus · Scripts · CSS · Hidden content · Forms · Buttons · Page decorations

BotForge automatically follows internal website links — discovering every page of your site and adding each one to the knowledge base.

Home
About Us
Services
Products
Pricing
Contact

Each discovered page is analyzed and added to the chatbot's knowledge base, enabling it to understand information spread across your entire website.

Refreshing Website Knowledge

Websites change over time. Whenever your content changes, refresh your chatbot's knowledge to keep responses up to date.

New services added Updated pricing New blog articles Changed contact info New products launched
Note: After adding new pages to your website, you must manually refresh the website training. The chatbot does not automatically pick up new content.

Best Practices for Training

Write Clear, Specific Content

Avoid vague descriptions. Specific content produces better chatbot responses.

"We provide many solutions."
"We provide custom web development, mobile applications, cloud consulting, and AI automation services."

Organize Your Website

Use proper headings and structured content. Well-organized pages are easier for the crawler to understand.

Include FAQ Sections

FAQ sections greatly improve chatbot performance because they already contain question-and-answer style content the AI can directly leverage.

Keep Website Updated

Your chatbot only knows what exists on your website. Always keep important business information current and retrain when content changes.

What the Knowledge Base Does Not Include

BotForge only processes publicly accessible website content. It does not collect:

  • Password-protected pages
  • Private dashboards
  • User account areas
  • Hidden admin pages
  • Internal databases
This ensures security and privacy are maintained while training your chatbot on your business content.

BotForge discovers and crawls all publicly accessible internal pages within the limits of your subscription plan.
Can I retrain my chatbot?
Yes. You can refresh the knowledge base at any time whenever your website content changes.
Does the chatbot automatically learn new pages?
No. After adding new pages to your website, you must manually refresh the website training to include the latest content.
What happens if my website is unavailable?
If your website cannot be reached during training, BotForge will pause the crawling process until the website becomes accessible again.
